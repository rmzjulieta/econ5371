# Structural Breaks in U.S. Female Labor Force Participation by Age Group

## Project description

This project asks whether the U.S. female labor force participation rate (LFPR) experienced structural breaks between 1948 and 2025, and whether those breaks occurred at different times for women of different ages. I analyze annual data for six age groups (16-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, and 65+) and for the aggregate rate (16 and older), which serves as a benchmark.

For each series, I first test for unit roots (ADF, KPSS, and Zivot–Andrews tests). Because the series are I(1) in levels, I apply three structural break tests to their first differences: a Chow test at candidate break dates chosen from the raw series’ figure, a QLR test that estimates a single break date from the data, and a Bai–Perron-type procedure that allows for multiple breaks. I then divide each series into periods at the estimated break dates, classify each period as trend-stationary or difference-stationary, and summarize it by its trend slope. Comparing the aggregate with the age groups shows whether the timing of the breaks differs across ages. 

## Data source

- **Source:** U.S. Department of Labor, Women's Bureau (underlying data: BLS Current Population Survey)
- **Series:** Labor force participation rate of women by age, annual averages, 1948–2025. Graphic updated February 2026.
- **URL:** https://www.dol.gov/agencies/wb/data/lfp/women-by-age
- **Access Instructions:** Data was downloaded on September 30, 2026, directly from the website cited above. I manually selected the seven age-group series in the interactive chart and then pressed the download button below it. The downloaded file is tab-separated and UTF-16 encoded, and is stored unmodified in "data/raw/".

## Replication instructions

Activate the conda environment and run the scripts in order from the repository root:

1. `python project/code/01_clean_data.py` — reads the raw file in `data/raw/`, reshapes it (years as rows), and saves `data/clean/lfpr_women_by_age.csv`.
2. `python project/code/02_summary_stats.py` — produces `output/table1_summary_stats.csv` (summary statistics) and `output/fig1_lfpr_by_age.png` (raw series' figure).
3. `python project/code/03_unit_root_tests.py` — ADF, KPSS and Zivot–Andrews unit-root tests in levels and first differences; saves output/table2_unit_root_tests.csv and output/table3_zivot_andrews.csv.
4.  `python project/code/04_break_tests.py` — Chow, QLR and Bai–Perron break tests on the first-differenced series, using functions.py (ECON 5371, Lab 3); saves output/table4_chow_tests.csv and output/table5_qlr_baiperron.csv.
5.  `python project/code/05_period_analysis.py` — splits each series at its break years and classifies each period as trend-stationary or difference-stationary; saves output/table6_period_analysis.csv.
6.  `python project/code/06_figure_breaks.py` — plots each series with its Bai–Perron breaks and period trends; saves output/fig2_breaks_by_age.png.
